    6. H.R. 2847

    H.R. 2847 was introduced on June 12, 2009 to make appropriations for the Departments of Commerce and Justice, and Science, and Related Agencies for the fiscal year ending September 30, 2010, and for other purposes. Pursuant to H. Res. 976 the House modified the Commerce-Justice-Science Appropriations Act substituting the "Jobs for Main Street Act of 2010" as Division A of the Act and the "Statutory Pay-As-You-Go Act of 2009" as Division B. (Regular Appropriations were included in Division B of Public Law 111-117.)
