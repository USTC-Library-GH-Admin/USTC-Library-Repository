Reference Questions

Q: Trying to find where Section 530 of PL 95-600 would be codified in the US Code, as well as if it has since been amended since 1978
A: Some laws are not codified. For example section 530. It’s section 530 of the Revenue Act of 1978. It’s technically codified in the note field of IRC 3401. But when it’s amended, Congress amends the Revenue Act of 1978 not the IRC.
For LH purposes you can look at the LH of the Revenue Act of 1978 (& the laws that amend the Revenue Act of 1978). Amendments specific to section 530 are also listed in the note section of 3401.

Related Q: For Sec. 530 in Lexis the Annotations section at the bottom, below Amendment Notes, includes a section called “Other Provisions” that includes the text of the statute as amended. Is it still part of the Code?
A: “Other Provisions” is something of an anomaly. Unlike other entries under the Notes field which tell you how the section was amended Lexis considers this "added value" of other provisions, which they interrupt as "related provisions." So, if you were interested in this aspect, you need to look at section 72 of title 26.
